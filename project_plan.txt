# Untitled Game
# Overview
Mix of American Final Fantasy/FF (NES) for overworld and Darkest Dungeon (combat).  Players create a party of 3 right at start of game.
 - Overworld 
   - Top Down RPG (like final fantasy) 
   - Random Enemy encounters based on your zone + tile standing on.
 - Combat
   - Turn based side view (similar to darkest dungeon)
 - Party
   - 3 Character Party (3 Heroes)
 - Art Style
   - I'll be doing sketches (mostly black and white art)
   - We will use placeholder graphics/audio for all assets until they are created.  Images can be just a white square with a gray border (stretched to size/shape).  Audio can be... well anything at this point (maybe just a soft noise appropriate for what it is.

Initial mockup of game can be full of placeholders... but would be nice to have things sized/positioned to fill in with art/assets.

# Combat
  Similar in style to darkest dungeon (side view). Enemies face each other. (again - similar to darkest dungeon aka DD)
  - Heroes stand in "slots" or positions (1-3) left side of screen.  The right position (3) is considered a place for more "tanky" heroes. the back (1) is attacked a bit less and better for healer/mages.
  - Enemies stand in "slots" or positions (4-7) right side of screen. The left (4) is considered a place for more "tanky" enemies. back (7) is attacked a bit less and is considered more protected by slots 4-5.

## combat start
  - each unit rolls their speed + random num to generate an initial turn order to populate player turn queue

## weapons 
  - all weapons will have 4 abilities
    - default ability - this is the ability the weapon can use no matter what slot the hero is in.
    - ability 1 - ability also available if hero is standing in slot 1
    - ability 2 - ability also available if hero is standing in slot 2
    - ability 3 - ability also available if hero is standing in slot 3


## combat abilities available to heroes during their turn of combat
  - weapon skill (default ability) (based on equipped weapon in slot corresponding to the slot your standing in).
    - Example: if hero is in slot 1, and has a sword equipped in inventory weapon slot 1, weapon skill will be the default ability (for the sword).
    - Example: if hero moves to slot 2, but has not equipped a weapon into inventory weapon slot 2, the primary weapons slot (the one unlocked at game start) is used.
  - weapon skill slot based
    - Example: if hero has sword equipped and is in slot 1, he might do a stab attack (sword ability 1)
    - Example: if hero has sword equipped and moves to slot 2, this ability will change to sword ability 2.
    - Example: if hero has sword equipped and moves to slot 3, this ability will change to sword ability 3.
  - class based skill (this skill never changes and is based on your class) ie: paladin might have a single target heal.
  - class based skill (slot based) skill that changes based your the slot the hero is standing in
    - Example: if fighter hero is in slot 1, he might perform a shout "buff"
    - Example: if fighter hero is in slot 3, he might perform a sweep bleed attack to front two enemy slots.
  - Move: move does not consume the turn but is cooldown based.
  - Trinket skill 1 (if available)
  - Trinket skill 1 (if available)
  - Combat item skill 1 (if available)
  - Combat item skill 1 (if available)
  - move (on a cooldown)
  - pass (moves your hero down the queue... you will still attack this turn but you move down a random amount of time)
	
# Weapon slots: weapon slots are special
  - there are 3 weapon slots (1,2,3) (two of them are locked at start of game)
  - the initial (and primary) weapon slot is that is unlocked is determined by the heroes "slot" when the game is created.
  - eg: hero in slot 1 starts with weapon slot 1 unlocked, they will use that weapon standing in any position/unless they unlock
  and equip a different weapon into weapon slot 2 or 3.
